Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy applies to “fasrc-rclone”, a private OAuth client used by Emily Silcock for academic research.

What fasrc-rclone is

fasrc-rclone authorises rclone, an open-source file transfer tool, to upload academic research data files to the Google Drive account of the person who authorises it. It runs on Harvard's FAS Research Computing cluster. It has a single user: the owner of the Google account it is authorised against.

What it accesses

With your permission, fasrc-rclone accesses files in your own Google Drive, in order to (a) upload research data files, and (b) list files already present so that work already uploaded is not repeated. It does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, Photos, or any other Google service.

Limited Use

fasrc-rclone's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the file-transfer function described above. It is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide that function, not used for advertising, not used to build profiles, and not read by any human except the account owner.

What it does not do

It collects no personal information. It has no other users. It uses no analytics or tracking. It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Storage, retention and revocation

Uploaded files are stored in the Google Drive account of the person who authorised the client, remain under their control, and may be deleted by them at any time. The OAuth token is stored in the rclone configuration file on the research cluster, readable only by the account owner. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
